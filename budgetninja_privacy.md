# BudgetNinja Privacy Policy

_Last Updated: 10/04/2025_

Welcome to BudgetNinja. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

BudgetNinja is designed to function primarily on your device without requiring the transmission of your financial data to external servers. We collect and process the following information:

*   **Financial Data:** Expenses, income, categories, and other financial information you enter in the app
*   **Device Information:** Basic information about your device required for the app to function properly
*   **Authentication:** If you enable biometric authentication, we use Apple's secure authentication frameworks to verify your identity

## How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

*   To provide and maintain the core functionality of BudgetNinja
*   To improve and optimize the application's performance and user experience
*   To enable optional features you activate, such as iCloud synchronization

## Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

*   **Local Storage:** Your financial data is primarily stored locally on your device
*   **iCloud Synchronization:** If you enable iCloud sync, your data will be encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account according to Apple's privacy and security standards
*   **Security Measures:** We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal data, including support for device-level encryption and biometric authentication

We do not have direct access to your financial data stored on your device or in your iCloud account.

## Third-Party Services

BudgetNinja utilizes the following third-party services:

*   **Apple iCloud:** For data synchronization between your devices (if enabled)
*   **Apple App Store:** For app distribution and updates

Each of these services is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention

Your data remains on your device and/or in your iCloud account until you choose to delete it. We do not retain your financial data on our servers.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

*   Access, update or delete your information within the app
*   Disable iCloud synchronization at any time
*   Request information about how your data is processed

## Children's Privacy

BudgetNinja is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
